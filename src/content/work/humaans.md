I helped rebuild AI Companion so people could ask questions across their HR data in plain English. Someone looking into how headcount changed can get a chart, while someone checking a company policy can follow a citation back to the original document. People can ask from Humaans or Slack.

I also helped build the Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. It lets AI tools work with HR data that people already have permission to access in Humaans.

I worked on AI credits so the credits customers spend more closely reflect their actual usage. I also built approval steps into AI workflows so people can review changes before records are updated, and helped make document uploads self-serve for customers.
